Design Outline Draft 1

A preliminary look for the launcher: a modern dark layout with warm, chunky, colourful tiles and playful sounds. Everything is open to change. Nothing here is approved yet.

To ask for a tweak, press Tweak next to any item and write what you want, or reply in chat with its number (for example "3.12 more orange"). I make the change in the Design Workbench, and nothing becomes final until you approve it there.
