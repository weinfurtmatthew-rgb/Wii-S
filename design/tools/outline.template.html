Design Outline Draft 3

Your inspiration pictures, answers and edits so far: frosted light panels over the focused game's art, a "Jump back in" card with your systems beside it, a navigation dock along the bottom of the top screen, and a large cover with details on the bottom screen. Everything is open to change. Nothing here is approved yet.

What you've decided so far

  1. Look: light frosted-glass panels and tiles over the focused game's art, blurred and darkened.
  2. Top screen: a big "Jump back in" card for your last game, a grid of systems beside it (A to Z), and recent games underneath.
  3. Navigation: a glass dock along the bottom of the top screen: Home, Apps and System between L1 and R1, then your app shortcuts.
  4. Bottom screen: a large cover of the focused game with time played, last played and emulator.
  5. Tiles: full box art in a thin ring of the console's colour, with a small console drawing in the top-right corner.
  6. Focus: the tile grows and bounces. No extra ring or glow.
  7. Colours: a grey accent and a grey active tab (kept, though white text on it is only 2.9:1), a teal Recently played colour and a deep indigo glow.
  8. Kept from Draft 1: centred rows, a bar before labels, and a sine-wave move sound.
